Zero tolerance for abuse

Names, usernames and shared plans can be seen by other people. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. Don't post anything hateful, sexual, threatening, harassing, or pretending to be someone else, and don't use MyGymBro to bully anyone.

  • We filter names, usernames and shared plans for offensive words.
  • You can report a person, a shared plan or a GymBro reply, and block anyone, right in the app.
  • We look at every report within 24 hours. We remove content that breaks these rules and close the account that posted it.

Subscriptions

MyGymBro works without paying. There are two optional subscriptions, each monthly or yearly, paid through your Apple Account: Pro (build and change your own plans, all-time history) and Pro + GymBro (everything in Pro, plus the AI coach). Both yearly plans start with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers; cancel before it ends and you aren't charged. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel, at least 24 hours before the period ends, in your Apple Account settings. Apple handles payments and refunds. How to cancel.

GymBro, the AI coach

GymBro is an AI, not a person, and it's for people 18 and over. Its answers can be wrong, incomplete or out of date, so check anything important before you rely on it. It isn't medical advice and isn't a substitute for a doctor or physio.

Train safely

Lifting has risks. The weights and reps in MyGymBro are suggestions worked out from what you log. Warm up, use good form, use a spotter or safety bars for heavy lifts, and stop if something hurts. If you have a health condition or an injury, check with a doctor before you start.

Be decent

Only share plans you're happy for others to copy, and keep your name and username your own. Breaking the rules above gets your account closed.

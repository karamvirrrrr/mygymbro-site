Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

This page explains what the MyGymBro app for iPhone and Apple Watch collects, why, where it goes, and what you can do about it. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed past.

The short version

  • We collect what the app needs to work. That's it.
  • No ads. No tracking. We never sell your data.
  • Apple Health data is never used for ads or marketing, never sold, and never stored in iCloud by us.
  • You can use MyGymBro without an account. Then your training stays on your iPhone and Watch.
  • GymBro, the AI coach, sends your messages and some training details to Anthropic to write a reply. Only after you agree, and only when you use it.
  • GymBro is for 18 and over. Apple checks your age for us and tells us only "18 or over: yes or no". Never your birthday.
  • You can delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app.

Who we are

MyGymBro is made by Kawin Pattiyawongse ("we" or "us"). We decide how your data is used, which makes us the data controller under laws like the GDPR and Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). You can reach us any time at kawin@mygymbroapp.co.

Using MyGymBro without an account

You can train without an account. Your plans, workouts and profile then live only on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and nothing about your training is sent to us.

Four things need an account, because they need a server: backing up and syncing your training across devices, plan share codes, friends and leaderboards, and GymBro, the AI coach.

What we collect and why

Your account

  • Email and password, if you sign up with email. Your password is handled by our sign-in service. We can't read it.
  • Sign in with Apple, if you use it. Apple gives us an ID for you, and your name and email if you choose to share them. If you pick Hide My Email, we only get a private relay address from Apple.
  • A display name and a username (like @ton.lifts). When you sign up we make a username from your name or the first part of your email. You can change both in Edit profile.

Why: to sign you in, keep your training tied to you, and let friends find you.

Your profile

The answers you give when you set up and in Settings: your goal, experience, the days you train and how long, your equipment, units, coach style, age, height, bodyweight, any sore spots or injuries you choose to note (and the short note you write about them), your default rest time, and your notification and leaderboard settings.

Why: to pick your plan and set the weight and reps for every set. If you're signed in, your profile is backed up so a new phone gets it back.

Your training

Your plans (ready-made, your own, imported or built by GymBro) and your workouts: exercises, sets, weights, reps, times, swaps and skips. Records and charts are worked out from these.

Why: this is the app. If you're signed in, each plan and workout is backed up to your account. A deleted plan or workout leaves a small marker (an ID and a date, no content) so an offline phone doesn't bring it back.

Apple Health

Apple Health access is optional. You choose each type on Apple's own permission screen and can change it any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > MyGymBro.

  • On iPhone, the app asks for what your Watch uses during a workout: to save workouts, and to read heart rate, active calories and walking and running distance. You pick which in setup. The iPhone itself doesn't read anything from Apple Health.
  • On Apple Watch, the app asks to save workouts, and to read heart rate, active calories and walking and running distance while a workout is running.

What happens with it:

  • Your Watch records your workout with Apple's workout tools, shows your heart rate and calories live, and saves the workout to Apple Health so it counts toward your rings. That saved workout is yours in the Health app.
  • When your Watch ends a workout it recorded, it gives the iPhone that workout's average and highest heart rate and its calories, and MyGymBro shows them on the workout. If you're signed in, those numbers are backed up with that workout, like the rest of it. Nothing else from Apple Health is sent to our servers.
  • Apple Health data is never sent to GymBro, the AI coach.
  • Apple Health data is never shown to friends or on leaderboards.
  • Apple Health data is never used for ads, marketing or data mining, never sold, never given to data brokers, and never stored in iCloud by us.

GymBro, the AI coach

GymBro is an AI, not a person, and it's for 18 and over (see Apple's age check). Before your first message the app asks you, once, to agree to what is sent. After that, nothing is sent until you send a message. When you do, the app sends our server:

  • the messages in that chat (up to the last 24)
  • your name, age, goal, experience, training days and session length, equipment, coach style and units
  • any sore spots or injuries you noted, and your note about them
  • a summary of the plan you're on, and your last 6 workouts (exercises, weights, reps and dates)
  • the app's list of exercises and today's date
  • the App Store's signed record of your subscription, if you have one (see Purchases)

Our server passes this to Anthropic's Claude API, which writes the reply, along with whether you're on Free, Pro or Pro + GymBro. If you ask for a new plan, a second request builds it. Your height, bodyweight, Apple Health data, Apple's age check answer and the subscription record are not sent to Anthropic.

  • We don't keep your chats on our servers. Your chats are saved on your iPhone.
  • Our server keeps a monthly count of your GymBro messages and plan builds, to apply the monthly limits, and short technical logs (which model answered and how long the request and reply were). No message text.
  • Under Anthropic's commercial terms, Anthropic may not train its models on what we send. Anthropic deletes it within 30 days, unless it needs to keep it longer to enforce its usage policy (for example, a message flagged as harmful).
  • GymBro can be wrong. Check anything important, and don't treat it as medical advice. If something hurts, stop and see a doctor or physio.

Apple's age check

GymBro, the AI coach, and the Pro + GymBro plan are for people 18 and over. To check, the app uses Apple's age range feature (Declared Age Range). The first time you open GymBro or a subscription screen, Apple asks you (or your parent, if you're in a Family Sharing group) whether to share your age range with MyGymBro.

  • What we receive: only whether you're 18 or over. Apple never gives us your birthday, and we never ask for it.
  • What we keep: "18 or over: yes", "no" or "don't know" (if you chose not to share, or Apple couldn't answer), and when we asked. On your iPhone, and, if you're signed in, on your profile, so our server can apply the same 18+ rule.
  • If you don't share, or you're under 18: GymBro stays off and only the Pro plan is offered. Everything else in MyGymBro works the same.
  • Changing it: on your iPhone, Settings > your name > Personal Information > Age Range for Apps. Then open GymBro and tap Try again.

Why: GymBro is an AI, and we offer it to adults only. Asking Apple means you don't have to tell us your age, and we don't have to keep it.

Friends and leaderboards

Anyone signed in to MyGymBro who types your exact username can see your display name and username, and send you a friend request. Nobody can browse or search a list of users.

Once you both accept, here is what a friend can see:

WhatCan friends see it?
Your display name and usernameYes
Your best set per exercise each day (exercise, weight, reps, and the estimated one-rep max worked out from them)Yes, while "Show me on leaderboards" is on
Your total weight lifted and number of workouts each day, added up into weekly boards, and how many workouts you did this weekYes, while "Show me on leaderboards" is on
The bodyweight in your profile, for pound for pound boardsOnly if you turn on "Share bodyweight" (it's off until you do). Turning it off takes it off the boards
Your full workouts, plans, profile answers, Apple Health data or GymBro chatsNo, never

"Show me on leaderboards" is on when you start. Turn it off any time in Settings. While it's off, the app stops posting your scores, and friends can't see the ones you posted before. You still see theirs. You can remove a friend at any time.

Block and report. Tap a friend to remove, block or report them. Blocking ends the friendship, takes you off each other's boards and stops them finding or adding you again. They aren't told. A report sends us your account ID, theirs, the reason you picked, and their name and username at that moment, so we can review it. They aren't told who reported them.

Plan share codes

When you share a plan, a copy of it goes to our server under a code like MGB-7KQ2PX. The copy holds the plan's name, days, exercises, sets, reps, rest, notes, and your display name as the author. It never holds your weights or your workouts.

Anyone signed in to MyGymBro who has the code can open and copy that plan. Treat a code like a link: share it with people you want to have it. Sharing the plan again updates the same code. To take a code down, delete your account or email us.

Purchases

Apple handles every payment. We never see your card or payment details. There are two paid plans, Pro and Pro + GymBro.

When you subscribe, restore, open the app, sign in or message GymBro, the app sends our server the App Store's signed record of your subscription. Our server checks Apple's signature, then keeps: which plan it is, the product, Apple's transaction numbers for it, when it renews or ends, whether it was refunded, and whether it's a test (Sandbox) purchase. Apple also tells our server directly when a subscription renews, changes plan, ends or is refunded.

Why: so the right plan and GymBro's monthly limit apply, checked with Apple rather than taken on trust. One subscription belongs to one MyGymBro account at a time.

Notifications

Rest timers and the trial reminder are local notifications, set up on your iPhone and Watch. We don't run a notification server and we don't send push notifications.

When you email us

If you write to us, we get your email address and whatever you send. We use it only to help you.

What we don't collect

Your location, contacts, photos, browsing, advertising ID or device ID. There are no analytics or crash-reporting tools from other companies in the app. If you save or share a workout card, it goes where you send it through your iPhone's share sheet. It isn't uploaded to us.

Where your data lives

  • On your devices: in the app's own storage on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Your sign-in is kept in the iPhone's Keychain. GymBro chats stay on your iPhone.
  • On our servers: if you're signed in, your account, profile, plans, workouts, share codes, friends, blocks, reports and leaderboard scores are stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, in Singapore.
  • With Anthropic: GymBro requests are processed by Anthropic, a US company, and deleted as described above.

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each account can only reach its own rows in the database. The few things other people can see (described above) go through narrow, checked functions on the server.

Who else handles it

We use two service providers. They process data only to run MyGymBro for us, under data processing terms that require them to protect it at least as well as this policy does.

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and the server functions behind GymBro, subscriptions and account deletion.
  • Anthropic: the AI behind GymBro, which writes its replies and builds plans.

Apple also handles some things directly with you: App Store purchases, the age range check, Sign in with Apple, Apple Health and notifications. Apple's own privacy policy covers those.

We may share data if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety. If MyGymBro is ever sold or handed to someone else, this policy goes with your data, and we'll tell you first.

No ads, no tracking, no selling

MyGymBro has no ads. We don't track you across other apps or websites, we don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it with data brokers or advertisers.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything in it (profile and its 18+ answer, plans, workouts, share codes, friends, blocks, scores, GymBro message counts, your subscription record): until you delete your account.
  • Reports you send: until we've dealt with them. If you delete your account, they stay without your account ID.
  • GymBro requests at Anthropic: deleted within 30 days, with the exception above.
  • Server backups and technical logs kept by our providers: a short time, until they roll over.
  • Emails to us: as long as we need them to help you, then deleted.

If you're not signed in, your data stays on your devices until you delete the app or sign out and erase it.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile > Settings > Delete account, then type DELETE. This deletes, for good:

  • your login, profile, display name and username
  • your plans and workouts
  • your share codes (they stop working)
  • your friends, friend requests and blocks (you disappear from friends' lists)
  • your leaderboard scores and daily totals
  • reports about you, and your account ID on reports you sent
  • your GymBro usage counts, your 18+ answer and our copy of your subscription record
  • the app's data on that iPhone, including GymBro chats

It can't be undone. It doesn't cancel an App Store subscription, so cancel that first (see how). Workouts your Watch saved to Apple Health stay in the Health app, because they're yours there. If you used Sign in with Apple, the app asks Apple to confirm, and we revoke MyGymBro's Apple sign-in as part of the delete.

Can't open the app? Email kawin@mygymbroapp.co from the address on your account and we'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • See your data and get a copy of it in a common format you can take elsewhere. Email us.
  • Fix it. Most of it you can edit in the app. For anything else, email us.
  • Delete it. In the app, or by email.
  • Change your mind. Turn off Apple Health access, notifications or leaderboards, or stop using GymBro, at any time. This doesn't undo what happened before.
  • Object or ask us to pause using your data while we sort out a question.

In the EU, the UK and similar places (GDPR), we use your data to give you the app you asked for, with your permission where the law needs it (like Apple Health, notifications and health details you choose to note), and to keep the service safe. In Thailand (PDPA), you have the same rights: to access, get a copy, move, correct, delete, object, restrict, and withdraw consent.

We'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to your data protection authority. In Thailand that's the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

Children

MyGymBro isn't for children. You need to be at least 13 to use it, or 16 if that's the age your country sets for agreeing to apps using your data. If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger, we'll delete it. Parents can email us. GymBro, the AI coach, and the Pro + GymBro plan are only for people 18 and over, checked with Apple's age check.

Security

We use encrypted connections, per-account access rules in the database, and keep server keys off your phone. No system is perfect. If something goes wrong that affects your data, we'll tell you, and the authorities where the law requires it.

This website

This site has no cookies and no analytics, and everything on it, the font included, loads from this site. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs, including your IP address, to keep the service secure.

Changes to this policy

Free, Pro, Pro + GymBro and paying

What's free, what's Pro and what's Pro + GymBro?

Free covers every ready-made plan, a number for every set, logging from the lock screen and Apple Watch, swaps, friends and leaderboards, and plan codes. If you're 18 or over, you also get 10 GymBro messages a month.

Pro adds your own plans, editing any plan, and charts past the last few weeks.

Pro + GymBro is everything in Pro, plus GymBro, the AI coach: 300 messages and 4 plan builds a month. It's for 18 and over.

Your history and records are never behind the paywall.

How much are the plans?

In the US, Pro is $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year, and Pro + GymBro is $7.99 a month or $39.99 a year. Both yearly plans start with a 7-day free trial if you haven't had one. Prices in other countries are set by the App Store, and the app shows yours before you pay.

Plans renew automatically until you cancel. Payment goes through your Apple Account.

How do I switch between Pro and Pro + GymBro?

In the app: Profile > Subscription > Change plan. Apple's own screen opens with both plans.

Moving up to Pro + GymBro starts straight away, and Apple refunds the unused part of Pro. Moving down to Pro, or between monthly and yearly, starts at your next renewal. Until then you keep what you have.

How does the free trial work?

Pick a yearly plan and you get it free for 7 days, then the yearly price is charged. If notifications are on, the app reminds you on day 5. Apple gives one free trial per person across both plans.

Don't want to keep it? Apple asks you to cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. You pay nothing.

How do I cancel?

In the app: Profile > Subscription > Manage subscription.

Or on your iPhone: Settings > tap your name > Subscriptions > MyGymBro > Cancel Subscription. If there's no Cancel button, it's already cancelled.

Your plan stays on until the end of the period you paid for. After that, your plans and history all stay. You can still train every plan, you just can't edit them, and GymBro goes back to the Free monthly messages.

I paid but Pro isn't on. How do I restore it?

Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you paid with, then go to Profile > Subscription > Restore purchases. There's also a Restore button on the Pro screen.

Still nothing? Email us with the date you paid. Never send us your card details.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles payments, so refunds go through Apple. Sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com, tap "I'd like to", then "Request a refund".

Your account

Do I need an account?

No. Without one, everything stays on your iPhone and Watch. You need an account to back up and sync, share plan codes, add friends, and use GymBro, the AI coach.

How do I delete my account?

Profile > Settings > Delete account, then type DELETE. It removes your login, profile, plans, workouts, share codes, friends and leaderboard scores for good, and clears the app on that phone.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription. Cancel it first (see above). Workouts your Watch saved to Apple Health stay in the Health app.

Can't open the app? Email us from the address on your account and we'll do it for you. More in our Privacy Policy.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and we'll email you a link to set a new one. If you signed up with Apple, use Sign in with Apple instead.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

What does MyGymBro do with Apple Health?

Your Watch records each workout, shows heart rate and calories while you train, and saves the workout to Apple Health so it counts toward your rings.

Health data is never sold, never used for ads, and never sent to GymBro, the AI coach. The details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I turn Health access on or off?

On your iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > MyGymBro. Switch each type on or off there.

How do I get MyGymBro on my Watch?

It comes with the iPhone app. If it isn't on your Watch, open the Watch app on your iPhone, find MyGymBro and tap Install.

Start a workout on your phone and MyGymBro opens on your Watch. Done, Skip, rest, and the weight and rep buttons all work from your wrist.

My Watch shows no heart rate or calories.
  • Only one workout can run on the Watch at a time. If the Workout app is recording, end it there and MyGymBro takes over.
  • Check Health access on your iPhone (see above). The app asks when you first tap Start.
  • Wear the Watch snug, a finger's width above your wrist bone.
I don't feel the tap when rest is over.

Turn on Prominent Haptic: open the Watch app on your iPhone, then Sounds & Haptics. Check that MyGymBro notifications are on too.

How do I log from the lock screen?

Start a workout and it shows on your lock screen and in the Dynamic Island. Use − and + for reps, then Done. Rest starts by itself and the next set is ready.

Don't see it? Make sure Live Activities are on for MyGymBro in your iPhone's Settings.

Friends, codes and GymBro

Can I hide from leaderboards?

Yes. Settings > Show me on leaderboards, off. Friends stop seeing your scores, and you can still see theirs.

How do I block or report someone?

Progress > Friends, tap their name, then Block or Report. Blocking takes them off your friends and boards and stops them adding you again. They aren't told. Reports come to us and we look at every one. You can also email us.

How do plan codes work?

Share a plan and you get a code like MGB-7KQ2PX. Anyone with the code can train your plan. Your weights and workouts stay yours. Codes need a signal to open.

Is GymBro a real person?

No. GymBro is MyGymBro's AI coach, and it's for people 18 and over. It knows your plan and recent workouts and can build or adjust a plan. It can be wrong, so check anything important, and it isn't medical advice. Pro + GymBro includes 300 messages and 4 plan builds a month; Free and Pro include 10 messages. They reset on the 1st.

Why is GymBro 18+, and how do you know my age?

GymBro is an AI coach made for adults, so it's for 18 and over. Everything else in MyGymBro is for everyone 13 and up.

We don't ask for your birthday. The first time you open GymBro or a subscription screen, Apple asks whether to share your age range with MyGymBro (a parent decides for a child in Family Sharing). We only learn whether you're 18 or over, and keep only that.

Under 18, or chose not to share? GymBro stays off and only Pro is offered. The rest of the app works as normal.

GymBro says to share my age range. How do I fix it?

On your iPhone: Settings > tap your name > Personal Information > Age Range for Apps, and check MyGymBro is allowed to see it. Then open GymBro and tap Try again.

It needs iOS 26 and an Apple Account signed in on the iPhone. If you're in a Family Sharing group, a parent or guardian may need to allow it.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes. Logging works offline and syncs when you're back online. Only GymBro and opening plan codes need a signal.
